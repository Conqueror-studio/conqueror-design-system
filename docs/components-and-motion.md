# Component and Motion Rules

## Button

Primary: ink background, ivory text; blue is reserved for an intentional emphasis variant. Secondary: transparent with ink 1px border. Minimum target 44×44px. Focus indicator must be obvious.

## Project card

Museum label, title, category/year, image. On hover: image scale max 1.03 and title/arrow moves 4px. Never hide required information on hover.

## Motion

Use restrained fade, mask reveal, image settle, and 10% max parallax. Honor `prefers-reduced-motion`; no motion is required to understand content. Opening sequence is skippable and persisted after first visit. Avoid scroll-jacking and cursor effects that impair normal interaction.
